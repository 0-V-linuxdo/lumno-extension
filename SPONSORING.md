# Sponsor Lumno

[简体中文](SPONSORING.zh-CN.md)

Lumno is free and open source software. Financial contributions help sustain maintenance, distribution, documentation, localization, security work, and community infrastructure.

## Official funding channel

Lumno accepts voluntary, one-time contributions through the project's official Stripe Payment Link:

Each contribution is HK$50. Stripe dynamically shows the payment methods available for the contributor's device and region. Cards, Apple Pay, and Link are active; Alipay and WeChat Pay will become available after Stripe approves them and when the transaction is eligible.

Stripe acts only as the payment processor. Contributions are general support for Lumno's open source work; they are not payment for a product, service, advertisement, or private deliverable.

## What funds may support

- Browser store, domain, hosting, and other project infrastructure costs
- Maintenance, design, documentation, localization, and security work
- Tools or services used solely for the open source project
- Payments or reimbursements for completed, publicly documented project work

All spending must have a clear connection to Lumno's open source mission.

## What sponsorship does not buy

Sponsorship does not provide:

- Guaranteed features, release dates, or roadmap control
- Custom or private development
- Priority support, response-time commitments, or service-level agreements
- Exclusive access, proprietary builds, or licensing exceptions
- Advertising impressions, endorsements, user data, or sales leads
- Administrative access to the repository, browser-store accounts, infrastructure, or project password manager

Feature requests and contributions from sponsors follow the same public process as everyone else's.

## Recognition

Lumno may thank contributors in project materials only with their permission. Recognition does not imply endorsement, partnership, or influence over project decisions.

## Transparency and commercial separation

Income is recorded through Stripe and administered for the benefit of the open source project. Lumno may publish aggregate funding and project-expense summaries without exposing private payment information. Project administrators must disclose conflicts of interest and must not approve unrelated personal or commercial expenses.

Any future commercial product, consulting engagement, hosted service, or client work must use separate finances, infrastructure, agreements, and paid operational accounts. Lumno's open source accounts, including any donated 1Password account, may be used only for the open source project.

For project decision-making, see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
