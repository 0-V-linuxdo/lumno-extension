# Built-in search icon sources

All runtime artwork in this directory is bundled locally. Brand names and marks remain the property of their respective owners.

- AI providers: version-pinned `@lobehub/icons-static-svg@1.94.0` assets from [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons) (`openai`, `gemini-color`, `doubao-color`, `qwen-color`, `yuanbao-color`, `minimax-color`, `deepseek-color`, and `kimi-color`).
- DuckDuckGo, Brave, and GitHub: matching color vectors from the Iconify `logos` collection. DuckDuckGo and Brave were cross-checked against their official brand/press resources.
- Bilibili, Ecosia, Zhihu, Juejin, X, and Wikipedia: matching brand vectors from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), retrieved through Iconify and frozen here.
- Tmall: the current Tmall vector wordmark sourced from Brandlogos; the square brand `M` is cropped from the same vector canvas for legibility in the search card.
- YouTube, Baidu, Bing, Google, Douban, Sogou, Taobao, and Reddit were already bundled as matching vectors before this audit and remain pinned locally.
